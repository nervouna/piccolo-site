Compatibility record

What each window action does when the focused window is in full screen or refuses to be resized, how long a shortcut takes from the key press until the window settles, and how much memory Piccolo uses. One section per macOS release, newest first.

macOS 27.0.1 (26A434)

Two displays, the primary one 2560 × 1440 and all of it available to windows; Stage Manager off and “Displays have separate Spaces” on. Piccolo was a Debug build of the development version after 1.0.0, with a window gap of 0 and the Fast window animation unless a table says otherwise.

Frames are written (x, y) width×height, in points. Left Half on the primary display is (0, 0) 1280×1440.

Full-screen windows

One window in full screen and focused. A second window, not in full screen, was focused before it; it stays in the desktop Space, off screen. Every action showed the notice “Full-screen windows can't be moved” and left the window in full screen at (0, 0) 2560×1440.

Windows that can’t be resized

A 640 × 420 window that refuses to be resized, at (120, 80). Piccolo moves it but cannot resize it, and shows no notice.

ActionNoticeWhat happened
Left HalfNoneMoved to (0, 0), still 640×420 (the target is 1280×1440).
MaximizeNoneMoved to (0, 0), still 640×420.
Arrange 2 in 1 × 2second window resizableNoneThe fixed window at (0, 0) 640×420, the other at (640, 0) 1920×1440: the divider follows the fixed window’s right edge and the other window fills the rest of the width. The space below the fixed window stays empty.
Move Divider Rightafter the arrangement“The divider cannot move further”Nothing changed: the fixed window cannot grow, so the divider stays where it is.

Resize increments of 7 × 15

A 637 × 420 window that resizes in steps of 7 × 15, like a terminal’s.

ActionNoticeWhat happened

In none of these cases did an action leave a window outside a display, and no action did nothing without a notice.
